Engine 8, you'll be the NFIC (New Fire Incident Commander) on arrival. Stage for offensive fire attack and primary search operations. ETA 0.4 minutes.  

Engine 15, responding, stage and establish backup water supply. ETA 1 minute.

Medic 12, responding. Stage and prepare Triage/Treatment Area. ETA 2.1 minutes.  

Unit 23, Officer Johnson, you have the initial Command role until relieved. Establish Incident Command and Scene Control. On-scene now.

Unit 47, Officer Smith, respond emergency traffic for perimeter control and evacuations. ETA 4.8 minutes.

Medic 5, stage and join Triage/Treatment team. ETA 5.1 minutes.

All other units switch to TAC 2. Use defensive operations and maintain safety distances due to potential collapse. Report PAR (Personnel Accountability Report) to Command on arrival."
